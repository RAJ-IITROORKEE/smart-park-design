# Brag Plan: SmartPark Revision D

## Planning rubric

1. **What is this?** An indoor ceiling-mounted parking sensor pilot built around a downward-facing HC-SR04, XIAO ESP32S3, and a custom carrier.
2. **Strongest claim:** The design is dimensioned for a ceiling up to 2.8 m above the floor; this is a design limit, not a field-test result.
3. **Visual hook:** The actual assembled CAD render, with its two ultrasonic faces and compact enclosure.
4. **What to show:** Actual product, electronics stack, OLED/status-only variants, PCB copper, and the repository’s fabrication and print downloads.
5. **Shortest satisfying length:** 20.5 seconds: enough for the product, the two build variants, and where to get the design files.
6. **Tone:** Polished; a restrained engineering product film with legible labels and precise motion.
7. **Audio:** A clean instrumental bed with three quiet, motion-matched accents; no narration.
8. **Share caption:** “SmartPark Rev D: an indoor ceiling-mounted parking sensor pilot, with the PCB and case files ready to inspect.”
9. **Real use flow:** Not a software flow. The designed sequence is mount above a bay → measure downward with HC-SR04 → report occupancy over LoRa to a separate receiver/gateway. Firmware and gateway are not included.

## The angle

A precise object study of one indoor parking bay sensor: the finished-looking enclosure opens into its actual electronics, resolves into two real case variants, then lands on the PCB and the build files. The footage presents the retained design as an unbuilt pilot, not a deployed or qualified product.

## Hook (0–3.2s)

Lead with the supplied product_oled.png CAD render. “One bay. Read from above.” appears beside it over a pale technical canvas. The top line identifies SmartPark Rev D and labels the work an indoor pilot.

## Key moments

- The supplied status-only housing and a dimension marker anchor the 2.8 m indoor ceiling design envelope.
- The real exploded enclosure and electronics-stack renders show how the carrier, modules, battery, and case relate.
- Two actual case renders arrive in sequence: OLED (7 printed parts), then status-only (6 printed parts).
- The actual PCB front/back previews lead to Gerbers, KiCad sources, and case-print files.

## Outro

Show the real PCB copper views, then hold the repository address with “Rev D · indoor pilot” and “Unbuilt design · firmware and gateway not included.”

## Tone and format

- Preset: polished
- Creative direction: calm, measured engineering product film
- Interpretation: long enough to inspect the real models; crisp type, short labels, restrained teal accents, and motion that feels deliberate rather than dramatic.
- Format: landscape — 1920 × 1080
- Duration: 20.5 seconds
- Visual identity: cool light background #EEF3F5; navy #183849; teal #168B8D; body #395461; fine rules #A9BEC3.
- Display font: Playfair Display; body: Montserrat; technical labels: IBM Plex Mono.
- Strongest source visuals: the supplied CAD assembly and PCB copper renders in previews/05-images/ and previews/01-pcb/.
- No live app UI exists. The product visual is the source CAD.
- Claims to avoid: built, deployed, production-ready, weatherproof/IP-rated, measured battery life, working firmware, included gateway, field performance.
